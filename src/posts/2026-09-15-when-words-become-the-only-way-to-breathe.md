**Sometimes, words are not written to be understood. They are written because silence has become too heavy to carry.**

There are things we never say.
Questions we never ask.
Pain we hide behind a smile.
People we desperately wanted to keep, but eventually had to let go.

We continue with life as if everything is fine. We work, we laugh, we talk, and when someone asks, *“Are you okay?”* we simply say, *“I’m fine.”*

But somewhere within us, there is a tired version of ourselves that is exhausted from pretending.

Perhaps that is why we write.

Writing gives us a place to confess what we cannot say aloud. To admit that we are hurt, that we miss someone, that we are afraid, or that some memories still quietly ache.

Some words are not meant for everyone.

**Sometimes, they are written for one person.
Sometimes, for the person we used to be.
And sometimes, simply because the heart needs somewhere to place what it can no longer carry.**
